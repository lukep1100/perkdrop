# Publicly advertised offers: editorial standard

PerkDrop may describe a venue's publicly advertised special as a discovery listing without claiming that the venue joined PerkDrop. This is an editorial workflow, not an automatic import or a PerkDrop redemption.

## Publication gate

1. Open the venue's own current offer page or original social post. Record its URL, capture time, exact price or benefit, eligible days and hours, location, exclusions, booking requirement, age limits and end date when stated. An aggregator is a lead, not final evidence. If material details cannot be verified, hold the listing.
2. Check the destination works, the offer has not ended, and no existing PerkDrop listing duplicates it. Avoid calling an ordinary price a discount without a supported comparison.
3. Set `offer_origin=public_source`, `fulfilment_mode=information_only`, `redemption_available=false`, and no exclusive, partner or business-approved status. Do not invent capacity, scarcity, savings, duration or a PerkDrop code. The customer action goes to the venue's source or booking channel.
4. Use PerkDrop-owned neutral graphics or a genuinely licensed image whose provenance covers website use. A venue's public image, logo or post is not automatically reusable. Link or use the social platform's native share feature for the original post; do not download and re-upload it into a carousel or Reel without permission.
5. Publish with the source visible, the public-offer label and plain conditions. Read back the live page on mobile, open its outgoing link, check category and location, and verify no claim flow is offered.
6. Recheck recurring specials before each promotional use and on a regular schedule. Remove or correct promptly if the source changes, the offer expires, or the venue disputes the facts.

Business-approved offers follow the separate approval and redemption checks. Claiming a business profile never retroactively approves an editorial offer or its imagery.

## Social format without venue media

Lead with a useful question tied to real plans, such as “Out in Adelaide on Thursday?” Use original PerkDrop typography and a short source-backed summary: venue, suburb, exact public offer, valid time and material catch. End with “Check current terms with the venue” and link to the PerkDrop listing or original source. For a video, use typography, maps made from PerkDrop's own assets and interface capture; do not present generic food footage as the venue's food.

Where one source is not enough for a compelling post, group only independently verified facts into a short local guide. Do not imply that editorial listings are PerkDrop partners, use partner badges, or promise claims through the app.
